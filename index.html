Blog

SEO notes & guides.

Short, practical reads on what actually moves rankings.

Local SEO · 3 min read

A Google Business Profile that ranks

Your profile is often the first thing customers see. Here’s what to fix first.

Read more

Start with a complete, accurate profile: primary category, services, hours and photos. Keep your name, address and phone number identical across every citation, ask happy customers for reviews and reply to each one. Consistent signals like these help you show up in the map pack for “near me” searches.

AEO · 3 min read

What is AEO and why it matters

AI tools answer questions directly. Answer engine optimization helps you be the source.

Read more

Write clear question-and-answer sections, use headings that match real questions and keep answers short and factual. Build brand mentions on trusted sites too. The goal is to be easy for both people and AI systems to quote.

Technical SEO · 4 min read

A technical audit that moves rankings

Most ranking problems start with crawling and indexing.

Read more

Check that important pages are indexable, fix crawl errors and broken redirects, and set canonical tags correctly. Then improve page speed and mobile experience. Re-check Search Console after each fix to confirm Google has picked up the change.

Off-page SEO · 3 min read

Ethical link building that lasts

Quality beats quantity. Links earned the right way hold up.

Read more

Focus on relevant sites, helpful guest posts, digital PR and genuine brand mentions. Avoid buying links or using spammy networks. A few strong, relevant links usually outperform dozens of weak ones.

GEO · 3 min read

GEO vs SEO: how to show up in AI answers

Generative engines summarize the web. Here’s how to be part of the summary.

Read more

SEO still matters because generative tools draw from pages that already rank and earn trust. Add clear definitions and well-structured pages, and keep your brand facts consistent across the web. Treat GEO as an extension of good SEO, not a replacement.

Analytics · 3 min read

Reading Search Console in 5 minutes

Four numbers tell you most of the story.

Read more

Clicks show visits, impressions show visibility, CTR shows how appealing your result is and average position shows where you rank. High impressions with a low CTR often mean titles and descriptions need work. Compare date ranges to spot what changed.
